# Legacy ready-made decks (not bundled)

These were bundled into the app before M7 (`prebuilt` format: front/back only). Since M7 the
app has no bundled decks; decks come from the Store and are published with `pnpm deck publish`
in the `ace-deck@2` format (BUILD_BRIEF_M6_M8 §7.2). The files are kept as source material for
converting to that format. Students who added them before M7 keep their copies (DECISIONS D-326).
